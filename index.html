About Me

I am currently a Visiting Scholar at the Institute of Operations Research and Analytics (IORA), National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Kim-Chuan Toh. Before that, I was a Research Fellow at IORA from December 2023 to July 2024.

I received my Ph.D. from the Institute of Applied Mathematics, Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Chao Ding. My thesis is titled "Perturbation analysis of composite matrix optimization and its applications". I received my B.S. in Information and Computing Science from Dalian University of Technology in 2018.

My research focuses on nonpolyhedral matrix optimization, including the variational properties of matrix optimization problems and efficient algorithms with convergence guarantees.
